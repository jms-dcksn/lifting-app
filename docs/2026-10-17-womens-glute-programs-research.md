# Published women's strength and glute programs

Researched 2026-10-17. These are useful programming references, not a proven ranking of the best programs. Vendor descriptions establish what is offered; they do not establish superior results. No subscriptions were purchased and no private program was accessed.

## Recommendation for the app proposal

Keep the existing three-day full-body framework. Booty by Bret explicitly recommends three full-body days for new subscribers, and Bodybuilding.com's Nia Shanks article provides a three-day full-body rotation. Matt Ogus's publicly displayed Vixen sample demonstrates that glute specialization can combine bridges, squats, hinges, unilateral work, and cable kickbacks while retaining upper-body work. This is design inspiration, not evidence that copying any commercial routine improves outcomes. [Booty by Bret](https://bootybybret.com/pages/get-started), [Bodybuilding.com strength training](https://shop.bodybuilding.com/blogs/training/these-workouts-are-how-strong-women-get-built), [Vixen](https://marketplace.trainheroic.com/workout-plan/team/vixen).

## Reviews and access limitations

- **Vixen:** the official page contains a featured Brittany Lesser testimonial and a customer-review section without visible review text or an aggregate score in the retrieved page. A targeted search did not identify a substantive independent Vixen review. The testimonial establishes an endorsement, not independent evidence of hypertrophy outcomes. [Vixen listing](https://marketplace.trainheroic.com/workout-plan/team/vixen).
- **Jeff Nippard:** the official glute product page exposes positive and critical customer comments, including complaints about exercise video links and missing substitutions. These are vendor-hosted, self-selected reports; useful UX signals, not controlled evidence. [Product reviews](https://jeffnippard.com/products/glute-hypertrophy-program).
- **Jamie Eason LiveFit:** the historical Bodybuilding.com program URL now redirects to the general membership page. Current availability and the original week-by-week plan were not verified from a first-party source. Do not describe old reuploads or forum copies as current official programming. [Historical URL and redirect](https://www.bodybuilding.com/fun/jamie-eason-livefit-trainer.html), [current membership](https://shop.bodybuilding.com/pages/bbcom-membership).
- **Stephanie Buttermore:** a direct check of her historical domain redirected to unrelated content. No claims about her program prescriptions are used. This is an access/identity limitation, not an assessment of her programming.

## Implications for the proposed template

These are design judgments drawn from the references above, not clinical or comparative efficacy claims:

1. Preserve the user's familiar three-day schedule and much of the current exercise inventory rather than importing a five-day commercial split.
2. Keep both hip-thrust/bridge work and squat/hinge/unilateral work; add cable kickbacks as trackable isolation, rather than replacing all compound patterns with isolation.
3. Make progression, effort targets, substitutions, and technique instructions explicit. A celebrity name or a long list of exercises does not substitute for these details.
4. Judge volume, loading, proximity to failure, and exercise claims against current primary research separately. None of these sales pages proves a named program is best in class.

No shared template, active user program, schema, or production data was changed by this research.
